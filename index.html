How it works

From search to return, in four steps.

01

Find a vehicle nearby

Search cars, motorcycles, boats and other motorised vehicles in your area, filtered by distance, price, and availability.

02

Verify and book

Confirm your identity and driving licence once, then book in a few taps.

03

Pick up and document

Meet the owner or a partner location, and record condition and mileage together.

04

Drive and return

Use the vehicle for your booking window, then return it and close out the agreement.

Trust and protection

Built around safety, from the first booking.

Every rental on Movanta is designed to be verified, documented and protected — not left to a handshake.

Identity verification

Every renter confirms their identity before booking.

Driving licence verification

Licence checks are built into the booking flow.

Digital agreements

Each rental is backed by a clear, digital agreement.

Before and after documentation

Photos, mileage and condition notes are recorded at pickup and return.

Timestamped records

Every step is time-stamped, creating a reliable record.

Support and dispute records

Documentation is available if a booking needs to be reviewed.

Insurance protection is intended to be provided through an external insurance partner. Coverage details will be shared ahead of launch.
